Vice Coast Cover Studio — turn your photo and a caption into multi-panel GTA-style cover art with a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, and export it as a PNG in 1080x1080, 1080x1350 or 1920x1080. Fully offline: photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded. Every sun, skyline and palm is drawn by the app itself — no game logo, artwork or font.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Load a photo into each panel (or use the built-in demo photos), write your caption, pick a layout, grade and post size, and export the PNG.
